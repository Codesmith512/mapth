A general style guide that I need to keep in mind as I write for this project.

# Settlements
Settlements represent fields of math - set theory, algebra, type theory, etc.

They are ranked by rigor, from least to most:
1. Village
2. City (Formal notation)
3. Capital (Proofs)

Each settlement requires
- How the people that live there view the world, including primitives
- Jobs that most of these people tend to have

# Roads
Roads connect fields of math, forming the basis for category theory.

They are also ranked by rigor:
1. Unpaved
2. Footpath (Formal notation)
3. Road (Proofs)

Each road requires
- A narrative about the people that use the road
- How old the road is, and why it was made
- Navigation signs
